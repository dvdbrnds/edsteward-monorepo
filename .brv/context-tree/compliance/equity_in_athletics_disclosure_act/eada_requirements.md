## REG-017 Equity in Athletics Disclosure Act (EADA) - January 2026

- Statute: 20 U.S.C. § 1092(g); 34 CFR 668.47
- Tasks: 0 requirements, 3 best practices

### Key Finding
The actual EADA requirement is to SUBMIT annual athletics data to DOE (participation rates, staffing, revenues, expenses). The 3 tasks in the database are all administrative best practices:
- Conduct Annual Compliance Training (training not mandated)
- Document Compliance Activities (documentation not mandated)
- Review Compliance Policies (review not mandated)

### Note
The core statutory requirement (annual data submission) is not captured as a task. Consider adding: 'Submit Annual EADA Report to DOE' as requirement.
