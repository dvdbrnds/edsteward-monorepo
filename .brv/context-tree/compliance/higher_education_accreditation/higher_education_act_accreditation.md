## REG-012 Higher Education Act Accreditation - January 2026

### Categorization
- REG-012: Higher Education Act: Recognition of Accrediting Agency or Association
- Statute: 20 U.S.C. § 1099b; 34 CFR Part 602
- Tasks: 3 requirements, 0 best practices

### Tasks (all requirements):
1. Gather Assessment Data
2. Prepare Self-Study Materials
3. Review Compliance with Standards

### Rationale
Accreditation is REQUIRED for Title IV eligibility. These tasks are mandatory activities imposed by accreditors during reaffirmation cycles. No best practices - all are requirements because:
- Maintaining accreditation is a condition of receiving federal student aid
- Accreditors require these specific activities
- Failure to comply results in loss of accreditation and Title IV eligibility
