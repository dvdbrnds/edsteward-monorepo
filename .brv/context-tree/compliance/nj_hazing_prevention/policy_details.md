## REG-024 NJ Hazing Prevention - FIXED - January 2026

- Statute: NJSA 2C:40-3; Timothy J. Piazza's Law (2021)
- Tasks: 6 requirements, 2 best practices

### Requirements
1. Anti-Hazing Policy Adoption (Timothy J. Piazza's Law)
2. Hazing Penalties Establishment
3. Policy Distribution
4. Hazing Definition Communication (per NJSA 2C:40-3)
5. Medical Amnesty Policy (Good Samaritan provisions)
6. Incident Reporting Mechanism

### Best Practices
- Hazing Prevention Education
- Organization Registration Review

### Criminal Penalties (NJSA 2C:40-3)
- 3rd degree crime if death/serious injury
- 4th degree crime if bodily injury
- Disorderly persons offense otherwise
- Org fines: $1k-$5k first, $5k-$15k subsequent
