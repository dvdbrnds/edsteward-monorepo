## EdSteward Demo Script Summary - January 2026

Created comprehensive "Day in the Life" demo script for EdSteward.ai with two personas:

### Demo Structure (20-25 minutes)
1. **Scene 1 (5-7 min)**: CCO Morning - Dashboard overview, compliance posture at a glance
2. **Scene 2 (5-7 min)**: Processing a Task - Regulation detail, assigning to field officer
3. **Scene 3 (5-7 min)**: Field Officer Experience - EMAIL ATTESTATION (the wow moment)
4. **Scene 4 (3-5 min)**: CCO Sees Results - Audit trail, proof of compliance
5. **Scene 5 (2-3 min)**: Big Picture - Scaling, time savings, closing

### Key Demo Personas
- **Clara Compliance (CCO)**: Chief Compliance Officer, coordinates 355+ regulations
- **Freddy Field (Field Officer)**: Subject matter expert, attests via email

### The "Wow" Moment
One-click email attestation: Field officer receives email, clicks "I Attest to Compliance" button, done in 5 seconds. No login required. Cryptographic token verification. Audit trail automatically updated.

### Key Messages
- CCO: "Coordinate, don't chase"
- Field Officer: "One click from your inbox"
- Auditors: "Defensible in seconds"
- Leadership: "One person can manage 355 regulations"
