# EdSteward Demo Script for CFOs

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes  
**Audience:** Chief Financial Officers  
**Key Theme:** Risk mitigation, accountability, audit trails

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

> "Compliance failures cost institutions millions in fines and reputational
> damage. The Clery Act alone can result in fines up to $69,733 per violation.
> The challenge is tracking WHO did WHAT and WHEN across dozens of federal and
> state regulations."

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview (1 minute)

**What to show:**

- Main regulation list with color-coded status badges
- Upcoming deadline indicators
- Category filters (Campus Safety, Financial Aid, etc.)

**What to say:**

> "This is your institution's compliance command center. Every federal, state,
> and accreditation requirement in one place. Green means compliant, yellow
> means action needed, red means overdue."

---

## 2. Deep Dive: Clery Act Example (3-4 minutes)

**Click into the Clery Act regulation and show:**

### A. Compliance Status Card (top of page)

- Overall status indicator (Compliant/Non-Compliant)
- Actions complete counter (e.g., "3/4 actions complete")
- Next deadline highlighted

**What to say:**

> "At a glance, leadership knows exactly where we stand. No digging through
> emails or spreadsheets."

### B. DRI Attestation Signature

- Show the green signature block with name, email, timestamp
- Point out the full date/time format

**What to say:**

> "This is the audit gold. When the Department of Education asks 'who certified
> compliance?' - here's the answer with a digital signature and timestamp. This
> person is on the hook."

### C. Compliance Tasks (expand the accordion)

- Show hierarchical task structure
- Point out parent tasks with sub-tasks
- Show completion checkboxes with signatures

**What to say:**

> "Complex regulations like Clery have dozens of requirements. We break them
> into trackable tasks. Each task shows WHO completed it and WHEN - down to the
> second."

---

## 3. 💰 The Audit Trail (2 minutes)

**Click on a completed task to open the detail dialog:**

### Show the Details tab:

- Task status with completion timestamp
- Assigned user
- Due date

### Show the Evidence tab:

- Uploaded files with uploader name and timestamp
- "This policy was uploaded by [Name] on [Date]"

**What to say:**

> "When auditors say 'prove you published this policy' - one click. The evidence
> is here, we know who uploaded it, and when. No more scrambling through file
> shares."

---

## 4. NEW: Delegation Without Risk (2 minutes)

**Show the Primary DRI dropdown (admin view):**

- Point to "Primary DRI" selector in regulation header
- Explain the delegation model

**What to say:**

> "The CCO can't personally manage every regulation. For Clery, we delegate to
> the Chief of Police. They become the Primary DRI - they get admin controls for
> JUST this regulation. They can manage tasks, assign field officers, submit
> attestations."

> "But here's the key: Every action is still tracked. The CCO can override
> anytime. And if something goes wrong, we know exactly who was responsible."

### Show Escalation Contact on a task:

- Open a task detail
- Point to the Escalation Contact section

**What to say:**

> "Every task has a clear escalation path. If the assigned person doesn't
> complete it, we know exactly who to escalate to."

---

## 5. Automated Notifications (1 minute)

**What to say:**

> "The system sends automated reminders as deadlines approach. 30 days out,
> weekly, then daily. No more 'I forgot' excuses. And we have a complete log of
> every notification sent."

---

## 6. Closing (30 seconds)

**Key benefits to emphasize:**

| For CFOs                  | EdSteward Delivers             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Reduce institutional risk | Clear accountability chain     |
| Audit readiness           | One-click evidence retrieval   |
| Liability protection      | Digital signature trail        |
| Operational efficiency    | Automated tracking & reminders |

**Closing statement:**

> "EdSteward turns compliance from a liability into a documented, defensible
> process. When regulators come knocking, you're not scrambling - you're
> clicking 'print report.'"

---

## Demo Checklist

Before the demo, verify:

- [ ] Server is running

- [ ] At least one task has evidence uploaded
- [ ] Primary DRI is assigned to show the feature
