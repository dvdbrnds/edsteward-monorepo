# Clery Act Meeting — Campus Police Team

**Meeting context**: Weekly Clery meeting with Police Chief and team. Goals: learn their current process, understand pain points, and demo EdSteward.

---

## Ask Today (In the Meeting)

- [ ] Walk me through the full annual cycle — when does each phase start and who drives it?
  - *Talking points: Jan–Mar is data collection (local PD requests, CSA reconciliation, DOE survey portal opens), Apr–Jun is classification and ASR drafting, Jul–Sep is review and legal sign-off, Oct 1 hard deadline for ASR publication and distribution, mid-Oct DOE Campus Safety Survey submission. Year-round obligations run parallel: daily crime log (2 business day entry window), timely warnings, emergency notifications, fire log, ongoing CSA training, prevention programs. Where do the year-round items fall in your workflow vs. the annual push?*

> Notes:

- [ ] What part of Clery compliance takes the most time or causes the most stress for your team?
  - *Talking points: chasing CSA reports, reconciling data from local PD and FBI UCR categories, VAWA crime classification gray areas, coordinating across Title IX and Student Affairs*

> Notes:

- [ ] If you could fix one thing about the current process, what would it be?
  - *Talking points: manual tracking, spreadsheet fatigue, evidence scattered across drives/binders/email, lack of year-round accountability from other offices*

> Notes:

- [ ] Who are all the people and offices that touch Clery work beyond your team?
  - *Talking points: Title IX (VAWA investigations), Residence Life (fire safety report, missing students), HR (employee training), Student Affairs (prevention programs, orientation), Facilities (geography, access control), Communications (ASR distribution, timely warnings), Counseling (voluntary reporting policy), Provost (study abroad geography, faculty CSAs), General Counsel (ASR legal review)*

> Notes:

- [ ] When Department of Ed shows up, how ready are you — and what takes the longest to pull together?
  - *Talking points: fire drill logs, CSA training records, evidence of prevention programs, MOU with local law enforcement, proof of ASR distribution, timely warning decision documentation*

> Notes:

- [ ] **Demo EdSteward** — does this match how you think about the work, or are we missing steps?
