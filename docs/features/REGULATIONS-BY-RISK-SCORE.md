## IRS Scoring Methodology

The Institutional Risk Score (IRS) is calculated using a 5-factor model (max 100 points):

| Factor | Max Points | Description |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Financial Penalty | 30 | Maximum potential monetary fines |
| Federal Funding | 25 | Risk to Title IV and other federal funding |
| Accreditation Impact | 20 | Potential effect on institutional accreditation |
| Reputational/Legal | 15 | Media exposure and litigation risk |
| Operational Disruption | 10 | Impact on daily institutional operations |

### Risk Level Thresholds
- **CRITICAL (90-100):** Immediate existential threat to institution
- **SEVERE (70-89):** Major regulatory action likely, significant penalties
- **HIGH (50-69):** Substantial compliance risk, potential for significant fines
- **MODERATE (35-49):** Notable compliance burden, moderate penalties
- **LOW (0-34):** Minimal immediate risk, standard compliance requirements
