# Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (2023 Update)

## Summary
Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (Title IX) prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex, including sexual orientation and gender identity, in education programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. All educational institutions that receive federal funds must comply with Title IX.

## Key Requirements

### Non-Discrimination Statement
Educational institutions must adopt and publish a comprehensive non-discrimination statement indicating that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex, sexual orientation, or gender identity in their education programs and activities.

### Title IX Coordinator
Each institution must designate at least one employee to coordinate compliance efforts and investigate complaints related to sex discrimination. This individual is typically referred to as the Title IX Coordinator. Institutions must now ensure the Coordinator has sufficient resources, authority, and independence to effectively oversee compliance.

### Grievance Procedures
Institutions must adopt and publish grievance procedures that provide for the prompt, equitable, and reliable resolution of student and employee complaints alleging sex discrimination. The 2023 update emphasizes trauma-informed approaches and enhanced confidentiality protections.

### Athletics
Title IX requires institutions to provide equal athletic opportunities for members of both sexes, including:
- Equal opportunities to participate
- Equitable allocation of scholarships
- Equal treatment in areas including equipment, scheduling, facilities, coaching, and publicity
- New protections to ensure transgender students can participate in athletics consistent with their gender identity, subject to specific eligibility criteria

### Sexual Harassment
Title IX prohibits sexual harassment, including:
- Quid pro quo harassment (conditioning an educational benefit on unwelcome sexual conduct)
- Hostile environment harassment (unwelcome conduct that is sufficiently severe or pervasive that it denies or limits equal access)
- Sexual assault, dating violence, domestic violence, and stalking
- Online and digital harassment that impacts the educational environment

### Pregnancy and Parental Status
Institutions cannot discriminate against students based on pregnancy, childbirth, false pregnancy, termination of pregnancy, recovery from these conditions, or related medical conditions. The 2023 update requires institutions to provide reasonable accommodations for pregnant students, including excused absences and extended deadlines.

## Enforcement
The U.S. Department of Education's Office for Civil Rights (OCR) enforces Title IX. Institutions found in violation may lose federal funding. The 2023 update strengthens monitoring and enforcement mechanisms.

## Exemptions
Religious institutions may be exempt from Title IX requirements that conflict with religious tenets, but must now formally document and publish the specific exemptions claimed. Military institutions and traditionally single-sex institutions have certain exemptions as well.

## Reporting Requirements
Institutions must maintain records of Title IX complaints, investigations, and resolutions for at least 7 years, and submit compliance reports as requested by the Department of Education. The 2023 update introduces new public disclosure requirements for statistical information on complaints and resolutions.
