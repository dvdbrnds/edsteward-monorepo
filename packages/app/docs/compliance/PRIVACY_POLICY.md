## 1. Introduction

EdSteward ("we," "our," or "us") provides a regulatory compliance management platform designed for higher education institutions. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our platform and services.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our customers, their users, and the data they entrust to us. This policy reflects our compliance with applicable privacy regulations including FERPA, GDPR (where applicable), and state privacy laws.

## 3. How We Use Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses

We use the information we collect to:

- **Provide Services**: Operate and maintain the EdSteward platform
- **Authentication**: Verify user identity and manage access
- **Communication**: Send service notifications, updates, and support responses
- **Security**: Protect against unauthorized access and security threats
- **Compliance**: Generate audit trails and compliance reports
- **Improvement**: Analyze usage to improve our platform

### 3.2 AI-Assisted Features

EdSteward uses artificial intelligence to:
- Analyze regulatory documents and extract compliance requirements
- Suggest task assignments and deadlines
- Identify potential compliance gaps

**Important**: AI analysis is performed on regulatory content, not personal information. Customer-specific compliance data is processed within isolated tenant environments.

### 3.3 Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data based on:

| Legal Basis | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Contract Performance** | Providing requested services |
| **Legitimate Interests** | Security, fraud prevention, service improvement |
| **Legal Obligation** | Compliance with applicable laws |
| **Consent** | Optional features, marketing (where required) |

## 4. FERPA Compliance

### 4.1 Our Role Under FERPA

EdSteward operates as a **School Official** under FERPA when processing student education records on behalf of educational institutions. We act under the direct control of the institution and use education records only for authorized purposes.

### 4.2 Education Records

When institutions use EdSteward to manage compliance involving student education records:

- **Access**: Only authorized users designated by the institution can access education records
- **Use**: Education records are used solely for compliance management purposes as directed by the institution
- **Disclosure**: We do not disclose education records to third parties except as directed by the institution or required by law
- **Re-disclosure**: We do not permit re-disclosure of education records without authorization

### 4.3 Institutional Responsibilities

Educational institutions using EdSteward remain responsible for:
- Determining which users have access to education records
- Ensuring appropriate use of education records within the platform
- Complying with FERPA notice and consent requirements
- Responding to student/parent requests regarding their education records

### 4.4 Our FERPA Commitments

EdSteward commits to:

1. **Direct Control**: Operating under the direct control of our institutional customers
2. **Legitimate Educational Interest**: Processing education records only for legitimate educational purposes
3. **No Commercial Use**: Not using education records for marketing or commercial purposes unrelated to our contracted services
4. **Audit Compliance**: Maintaining records of access to education records for auditing purposes
5. **Breach Notification**: Promptly notifying institutions of any unauthorized access to education records

### 4.5 Data Handling Agreement

Our contracts with educational institutions include provisions addressing:
- Scope of access to education records
- Permitted uses of education records
- Security requirements
- Breach notification procedures
- Data return and deletion upon contract termination

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We Do Not Sell Personal Information

EdSteward does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

### 5.2 Service Providers

We share information with service providers who assist in operating our platform:

| Provider Type | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **Cloud Infrastructure (AWS)** | Hosting and storage | All data (encrypted) |
| **Database (Neon)** | Data storage | All data (encrypted) |
| **AI Services (OpenAI, Anthropic)** | Document analysis | Regulatory content only (no PII) |
| **Email Services** | Notifications | Email addresses, names |

All service providers are bound by data processing agreements requiring appropriate security measures.

### 5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by:
- Valid legal process (subpoena, court order)
- Government requests where legally compelled
- Protection of our legal rights
- Emergency situations involving safety

### 5.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, customer data may be transferred. We will provide notice and choices consistent with applicable law.

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures including:

| Control | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Encryption in Transit** | TLS 1.2+ for all connections |
| **Encryption at Rest** | AES-256 encryption |
| **Access Controls** | Role-based access, MFA available |
| **Tenant Isolation** | Database-per-tenant architecture |
| **Audit Logging** | Comprehensive activity logging |
| **Vulnerability Management** | Regular scanning and patching |

### 6.2 Security Certifications

- **Infrastructure**: AWS (SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP)
- **Database**: Neon (SOC 2 Type 2)
- **EdSteward**: HECVAT compliant (self-assessed)

## 8. Your Rights

### 8.1 All Users

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request information about data we hold about you
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Export**: Request a copy of your data in portable format

### 8.2 Additional Rights (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, you also have the right to:
- **Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of processing
- **Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- **Lodge Complaint**: File a complaint with a supervisory authority

### 8.3 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have additional rights including:
- Right to know categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of "sale" of personal information (we do not sell)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

### 8.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

**For End Users**: Contact your institution's EdSteward administrator, who controls your account and data.

**For Institution Administrators**: Contact us at privacy@edsteward.ai with your request.

**Response Time**: We respond to verified requests within 30 days (or sooner where required by law).

## 9. Multi-Tenant Architecture

### 9.1 Data Isolation

EdSteward uses a database-per-tenant architecture ensuring:
- Each institution's data is stored in a separate database
- No cross-tenant data access is possible
- Tenant context is validated on every request

### 9.2 Administrator Control

Institution administrators control:
- User access and permissions within their tenant
- Data retention within their tenant
- SSO/authentication configuration
- Feature enablement

## 10. Children's Privacy

EdSteward is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

**Note**: Institutions using EdSteward to manage compliance related to students should ensure appropriate protections are in place per FERPA and institutional policies.
