## REG-027 COPPA - FIXED - January 2026

- Statute: 15 U.S.C. § 6501; 16 CFR Part 312
- Tasks: 7 requirements, 1 best practice

### Requirements
1. COPPA Applicability Determination - identify child-directed services
2. Online Privacy Notice Posting (§ 312.4)
3. Direct Notice to Parents (§ 312.4)
4. Verifiable Parental Consent (§ 312.5) - CRITICAL
5. Parental Access Rights (§ 312.6)
6. Data Security Procedures (§ 312.8)
7. Data Retention Limits (§ 312.10)

### Higher Ed Context
Applies if institution operates websites/services:
- Directed at children under 13
- That knowingly collect personal info from children under 13
Examples: Summer camps, K-12 outreach programs, children's museums
