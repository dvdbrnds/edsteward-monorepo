## REG-023 Drug Free Schools Act - FIXED - January 2026

- Statute: 20 U.S.C. § 1011i; 34 CFR Part 86
- Tasks: 10 requirements, 2 best practices

### Requirements
1. Annual Policy Distribution - to all students and employees
2. Standards of Conduct Statement - prohibiting unlawful possession/use/distribution
3. Legal Sanctions Description - local, state, federal
4. Health Risks Description
5. Available Resources Information - counseling, treatment, rehab
6. Institutional Sanctions Statement - up to expulsion/termination
7. Biennial Program Review - assess effectiveness
8. Violation and Fatality Tracking
9. Sanctions Consistency Review
10. Records Availability - to Secretary and public upon request

### Best Practices
- Prevention Programming
- Staff Training

### Key Deadline
Biennial review must be completed every two years
