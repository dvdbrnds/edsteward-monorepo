## REG-028 ECPA - FIXED - January 2026

- Statute: 18 U.S.C. §§ 2510-2523 (Wiretap); 18 U.S.C. §§ 2701-2713 (Stored Communications)
- Tasks: 4 requirements, 2 best practices

### Nature of Law
ECPA is a PROHIBITION statute - primarily tells you what NOT to do:
- Don't intercept communications without consent
- Don't access stored communications without authorization

### Requirements (policies to comply with prohibitions)
1. Electronic Monitoring Policy
2. User Consent for Monitoring (consent exception to Wiretap Act)
3. Stored Communications Access Policy
4. Law Enforcement Request Procedures

### Best Practices
- IT Staff ECPA Training
- Banner Notices for monitoring disclosure
