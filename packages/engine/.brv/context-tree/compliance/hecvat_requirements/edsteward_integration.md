HECVAT integration requirements for EdSteward compliance tracker:

EDUCAUSE HECVAT (Higher Education Community Vendor Assessment Toolkit) is a comprehensive vendor risk assessment questionnaire used by colleges and universities to evaluate technology service providers. Key points for integration:

**HECVAT 4 Requirements:**
- Single comprehensive questionnaire covering cybersecurity, privacy, IT accessibility, and compliance
- Includes AI-specific assessment questions for modern solutions
- Must be completed annually by vendors
- Can be shared with any higher education institution without modification
- No cost for higher education institutions and their vendors to use

**Integration Strategy for EdSteward:**
- Need to complete HECVAT 4 as vendor serving higher education market
- Should integrate HECVAT compliance tracking into MCP validation system
- Could position EdSteward as HECVAT-compliant solution for other institutions
- May need specific license from EDUCAUSE if integrating HECVAT into platform for third parties

**Key Categories Covered:**
- Organization policies and procedures
- Cybersecurity controls and practices
- Data protection and privacy measures
- IT accessibility compliance
- AI governance and risk management
- Incident response and business continuity
- Regulatory compliance (FERPA, HIPAA where applicable)

**Commercial Opportunity:**
- HECVAT completion positions vendor favorably for higher education sales
- Many institutions require HECVAT before procurement decisions
- Can accelerate sales cycles in higher education market
- Demonstrates commitment to higher education-specific requirements
