EdSteward Institutional Risk Scoring Framework (IRS) - A 1-100 scoring system for regulations based on consequences of non-compliance, separate from L.O.V.V. validation levels:

**5 Scoring Components:**
1. Financial Penalty Potential (0-30 pts) - max fines like Clery $14M
2. Federal Funding Risk (0-25 pts) - Title IV eligibility loss
3. Accreditation Impact (0-20 pts) - accreditation loss pathway
4. Reputational/Legal Exposure (0-15 pts) - headlines, lawsuits
5. Operational Disruption (0-10 pts) - program suspensions

**Risk Levels:**
- CRITICAL (90-100): Institution-ending - Clery Act (96), Title IV (96), Title IX (88), FERPA (85)
- SEVERE (70-89): Major financial impact - Title VI (77), ADA (74), VAWA (77)
- HIGH (50-69): Significant penalties - HEOA (52), Gainful Employment (60)
- MODERATE (30-49): Notable fines - Age Discrimination (40)
- LOW (1-29): Minor penalties - Textbook Information (29)

**Database fields:** risk_score, risk_level, risk_financial, risk_funding, risk_accreditation, risk_reputation, risk_operational, risk_notes, risk_last_assessed

**Key insight:** L.O.V.V. measures certainty of validation; IRS measures severity of consequences. A regulation can be L.O.V.V. Level A (easy to validate) but CRITICAL risk.
