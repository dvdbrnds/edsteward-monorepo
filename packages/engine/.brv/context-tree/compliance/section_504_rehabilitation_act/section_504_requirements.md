## REG-018 Section 504 of The Rehabilitation Act of 1973 - January 2026

- Statute: 29 U.S.C. § 701; 34 CFR Part 104
- Tasks: 0 requirements, 3 best practices

### Key Finding
Section 504 has actual requirements similar to ADA (34 CFR Part 104):
- Coordinator designation (for 15+ employees)
- Grievance procedure
- Self-evaluation
- Notice to participants

But the 3 tasks in database are administrative best practices, not the statutory requirements. Consider adding actual 504 requirements.
