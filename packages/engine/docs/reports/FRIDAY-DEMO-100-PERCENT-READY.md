## 📋 WHAT EACH REGULATION INCLUDES:

### 1. **Summary** (Prominent Display)
Clean, professional 1-2 paragraph summary of what the regulation requires.

**Example (FERPA):**
> "FERPA protects privacy of student education records and gives parents/eligible students rights to inspect records, request amendments, control disclosure of personally identifiable information, and file complaints. Institutions must provide annual notification of FERPA rights, respond to record requests within 45 days, and obtain consent before disclosing records except in specific circumstances."

### 2. **Full Regulatory Text** (Differential View)
Complete CFR or USC text showing:
- Official citations (e.g., "34 CFR Part 99")
- Section numbers and titles
- Complete regulatory language
- 786-2,531 characters per regulation

### 3. **Compliance Requirements** (Formatted Sections)
Detailed markdown-formatted compliance steps:
- Organized by category (Annual Notification, Record Access, etc.)
- Bullet points for each requirement
- Action-oriented language
- 537-1,277 characters per regulation

**Example (FERPA Requirements):**
```markdown
### FERPA Compliance Requirements

**Annual Notification:**
- Notify all eligible students of FERPA rights annually
- Include right to inspect and review education records
- Include right to request amendment of records
...
```

### 4. **Filing Deadlines** (Structured JSON)
2-4 deadlines per regulation with:
- Type (Annual, Biennial, Ongoing, As Requested, Immediate)
- Description (what the deadline is for)
- Date (specific or general timeframe)
- Recurring flag (true/false)

**Example (Clery Deadlines):**
