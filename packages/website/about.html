Why we built EdSteward

Higher education institutions face a unique regulatory burden. From FERPA and Title IX to the Clery Act and state-specific requirements, compliance teams juggle hundreds of obligations with limited resources and fragmented tools.

EdSteward was born from firsthand experience with this challenge. We saw compliance officers tracking regulations in spreadsheets, chasing attestations via email, and spending months preparing for audits that should take days.

We built a platform that automates the tedious parts — regulation monitoring, task generation, evidence collection — so compliance professionals can focus on strategy, risk reduction, and institutional improvement.
