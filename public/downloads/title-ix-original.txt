# Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972

## Summary
Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (Title IX) prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex in education programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. All educational institutions that receive federal funds must comply with Title IX.

## Key Requirements

### Non-Discrimination Statement
Educational institutions must adopt and publish a non-discrimination statement indicating that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex in their education programs and activities.

### Title IX Coordinator
Each institution must designate at least one employee to coordinate compliance efforts and investigate complaints related to sex discrimination. This individual is typically referred to as the Title IX Coordinator.

### Grievance Procedures
Institutions must adopt and publish grievance procedures that provide for the prompt and equitable resolution of student and employee complaints alleging sex discrimination.

### Athletics
Title IX requires institutions to provide equal athletic opportunities for members of both sexes, including:
- Equal opportunities to participate
- Equitable allocation of scholarships
- Equal treatment in areas including equipment, scheduling, facilities, coaching, and publicity

### Sexual Harassment
Title IX prohibits sexual harassment, including:
- Quid pro quo harassment (conditioning an educational benefit on unwelcome sexual conduct)
- Hostile environment harassment (unwelcome conduct that is so severe, pervasive, and objectively offensive that it denies equal access)
- Sexual assault, dating violence, domestic violence, and stalking

### Pregnancy and Parental Status
Institutions cannot discriminate against students based on pregnancy, childbirth, false pregnancy, termination of pregnancy, or recovery from these conditions.

## Enforcement
The U.S. Department of Education's Office for Civil Rights (OCR) enforces Title IX. Institutions found in violation may lose federal funding.

## Exemptions
Religious institutions may be exempt from Title IX requirements that conflict with religious tenets. Military institutions and traditionally single-sex institutions have certain exemptions as well.

## Reporting Requirements
Institutions must maintain records of Title IX complaints, investigations, and resolutions, and submit compliance reports as requested by the Department of Education.
