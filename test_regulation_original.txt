# Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972

## Summary
Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (Title IX) prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex in education programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. All educational institutions that receive federal funds must comply with Title IX.

## Key Requirements

### Non-Discrimination Statement
Educational institutions must adopt and publish a non-discrimination statement indicating that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex in their education programs and activities.

### Title IX Coordinator
Educational institutions must designate at least one employee to coordinate compliance efforts, including:
- Investigating complaints
- Monitoring outcomes
- Identifying patterns
- Assessing effects on campus climate

### Grievance Procedures
Educational institutions must adopt and publish grievance procedures that provide for prompt and equitable resolution of gender discrimination complaints, sexual harassment complaints, and sexual violence complaints.

### Athletics
Educational institutions must provide equal athletic opportunities for members of both sexes. This includes:
- Equal opportunities to participate
- Equal allocation of athletic scholarships
- Equal treatment in areas such as equipment, scheduling, facilities, etc.

### Sexual Harassment
Educational institutions must take prompt and effective action to:
- End sexual harassment and sexual violence
- Prevent its recurrence
- Address its effects

### Retaliation Prohibition
Retaliation against individuals who file Title IX complaints or participate in a Title IX investigation is prohibited.

## Application

This regulation applies to all aspects of education including:
- Admissions and recruitment
- Education programs and activities
- Course offerings and access
- Counseling
- Financial assistance
- Employment assistance
- Health and insurance benefits and services
- Housing
- Athletics
- Employment

## Reporting Requirements

Educational institutions must:
- Maintain records of all Title IX complaints and their resolution
- Submit annual reports detailing compliance activities
- Cooperate with OCR investigations
- Provide information to the Department of Education upon request

## Enforcement

The Office for Civil Rights (OCR) within the Department of Education enforces Title IX. Enforcement actions may include:
- Voluntary resolution agreements
- Withholding of federal funds
- Referral to the Department of Justice for litigation
