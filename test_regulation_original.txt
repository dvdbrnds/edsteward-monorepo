# Title IX Compliance Requirements

## Overview

Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex in education programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. All higher education institutions that receive federal funding must comply with Title IX regulations.

## Key Requirements

1. **Non-Discrimination Policy**: Institutions must implement a written policy stating that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex in their educational programs and activities.

2. **Title IX Coordinator**: Institutions must designate at least one employee to coordinate Title IX compliance efforts, including investigating complaints.

3. **Grievance Procedures**: Institutions must adopt and publish grievance procedures providing for the prompt and equitable resolution of student and employee sex discrimination complaints.

4. **Notification Requirements**: Institutions must notify all students, employees, applicants, and parents that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex.

5. **Training**: Institutions must ensure that Title IX coordinators, investigators, decision-makers, and any person who facilitates an informal resolution process receive training on Title IX regulations.

## Reporting Requirements

Institutions must maintain records of all Title IX complaints, investigations, and resolutions for a period of seven years. Annual reports must be submitted to the Department of Education summarizing Title IX activities.

## Enforcement

The Office for Civil Rights (OCR) within the Department of Education is responsible for enforcing Title IX. Institutions found in violation may lose federal funding.

## Compliance Timeline

- Initial Policy Implementation: Within 60 days of the effective date
- Coordinator Designation: Within 30 days of the effective date
- Training Completion: Within 90 days of the effective date
- Full Documentation System: Within 120 days of the effective date
