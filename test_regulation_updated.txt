# Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (2023 Update)

## Summary
Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (Title IX) prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex, including sexual orientation and gender identity, in education programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. All educational institutions that receive federal funds must comply with Title IX.

## Key Requirements

### Non-Discrimination Statement
Educational institutions must adopt and publish a comprehensive non-discrimination statement indicating that they do not discriminate on the basis of sex, sexual orientation, or gender identity in their education programs and activities.

### Title IX Coordinator
Educational institutions must designate at least one employee to coordinate compliance efforts, including:
- Investigating complaints
- Monitoring outcomes
- Identifying patterns
- Assessing effects on campus climate
- Publishing annual reports on Title IX activities
- Conducting regular training sessions for staff and students

### Grievance Procedures
Educational institutions must adopt and publish grievance procedures that provide for prompt and equitable resolution of gender discrimination complaints, sexual harassment complaints, and sexual violence complaints. The 2023 update requires:
- Clear timelines for investigation completion (60 days recommended)
- Transparent communication throughout the process
- Options for informal resolution when appropriate
- Trauma-informed practices for all participants

### Athletics
Educational institutions must provide equal athletic opportunities for members of all sexes. This includes:
- Equal opportunities to participate
- Equal allocation of athletic scholarships
- Equal treatment in areas such as equipment, scheduling, facilities, etc.
- Annual equity assessments with published results

### Sexual Harassment and Misconduct
Educational institutions must take prompt and effective action to:
- End sexual harassment and sexual violence
- Prevent its recurrence
- Address its effects
- Provide supportive measures regardless of formal complaint status
- Conduct climate surveys every two years

### Pregnancy and Parental Status
Educational institutions must:
- Provide accommodations for pregnant students
- Allow excused absences for pregnancy-related medical conditions
- Provide lactation spaces and break time
- Ensure parental leave policies apply equally regardless of gender

### Retaliation Prohibition
Retaliation against individuals who file Title IX complaints, participate in a Title IX investigation, or advocate for rights protected under Title IX is prohibited. The updated rule strengthens protections for those who report violations.

## Application

This regulation applies to all aspects of education including:
- Admissions and recruitment
- Education programs and activities
- Course offerings and access
- Counseling
- Financial assistance
- Employment assistance
- Health and insurance benefits and services
- Housing
- Athletics
- Employment
- Study abroad programs
- Digital and online learning environments

## Reporting Requirements

Educational institutions must:
- Maintain records of all Title IX complaints and their resolution for 7 years
- Submit annual reports detailing compliance activities
- Cooperate with OCR investigations
- Provide information to the Department of Education upon request
- Publicly publish Title IX statistics on institutional websites
- Document training materials and attendance records

## Enforcement

The Office for Civil Rights (OCR) within the Department of Education enforces Title IX. Enforcement actions may include:
- Voluntary resolution agreements
- Withholding of federal funds
- Referral to the Department of Justice for litigation
- Public notification of non-compliance findings
